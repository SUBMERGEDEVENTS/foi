Who We Are

A Community United for a Lake

Lake Istokpoga is Florida's fifth-largest lake and one of its most ecologically rich -- home to trophy bass, rare birds like the Snail Kite, and a watershed that sustains the entire Highlands County community.

Friends of Istokpoga Lake Association was founded in 1998 by local residents who saw the pressures of development and agricultural runoff threatening what they loved. We've been monitoring water quality, advocating for sound lake management, and educating the public ever since.

Our members include anglers, birders, kayakers, homeowners, and farmers -- anyone who has a stake in the lake's future.

What We Do

Three Pillars of Stewardship

🔬

Water Quality Monitoring

We track nutrient levels, algae blooms, and water clarity throughout the year, reporting findings to SFWMD and state environmental agencies to keep Istokpoga's water clean.

🦅

Wildlife Habitat

From nesting Ospreys to the federally threatened Snail Kite, we advocate for the preservation of shoreline vegetation, nesting areas, and the aquatic plants that support a complete food chain.

📢

Public Advocacy

We attend water management hearings, comment on permit applications, and work alongside county commissioners to ensure development decisions consider the lake's long-term health.
